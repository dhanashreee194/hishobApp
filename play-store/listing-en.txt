Title
Maza Hishob

Short description
Offline Marathi accounts for mill, vegetables, milk and credit.

Full description
Maza Hishob (माझा हिशोब) is an offline-first ledger for a household business that runs a flour mill, sells vegetables, buys milk, and tracks customer credit.

Keep daily sales, expenses, milk purchases, flour grinding, and udhari on the phone. Totals use exact rupees. Dates are shown as DD/MM/YYYY and amounts use Indian grouping.

Partner settlement shows the shared net, the partner share, payments, and what is still payable. Reports cover daily, monthly, yearly, and progressive periods.

The app works without a network. Optional phone sign-in can sync and back up to your own Supabase project. Local backup files are encrypted. An optional PIN or fingerprint locks the app when it opens.

Deleted money records stay recoverable from Settings. Nothing in this listing replaces your own review on a real phone before a production release.
