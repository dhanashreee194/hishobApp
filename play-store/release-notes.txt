Internal testing only. Do not promote this build to production until the owner has used it on a real device for about a week without issues.

Version 1.0.0 (versionCode 1)
- Optional PIN or fingerprint lock when the app opens
- Deleted money records can be restored from Settings
- Local backup files are encrypted
- Marathi labels, DD/MM/YYYY dates, and Indian rupee grouping
- Long lists virtualize, and customer, date, and grain lookups are indexed

Play Console upload and production promotion are not done from this workspace. They need the owner's Play Console account. CodePush is not included; small script fixes still need a store build until an App Center app exists.

The release keystore and android/keystore.properties stay on this machine and are gitignored. Back them up before reinstalling the computer. Losing them blocks later updates of this app on Play.
